# Clinical Software Constitution

## Core principles

- Protect patient confidentiality.
- Do not expose PHI/PII in prompts, logs, screenshots, tests, or examples.
- Do not perform destructive actions without explicit verification and rollback.
- Do not treat dashboard visibility as proof of underlying file or data integrity.
- Require validation evidence for sync, backup, cleanup, schema, and production-service changes.
- Maintain implementation logs and resume markers.
